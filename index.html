Type-2 Diabetes Medication for Asthma and COPD Exacerbations

This dashboard provides insights into the relationship between Type-2 Diabetes medications and asthma/COPD exacerbations. Use the filters to explore the data and visualise trends.

Data are based on the literature published up to July 2026. The data will be regularly updated.

Filters

Filter by:

  • COPD = Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease
  • OCS = Oral Corticosteroid
  • GLP-1 RA = GLP-1 Receptor Agonists
  • SGLT2i = SGLT-2 inhibitors
  • DPP-4i = DPP-4 inhibitors
  • HR = Hazard Ratio
  • IRR = Incidence Rate Ratio
  • RR = Relative Risk
  • OR = Odds Ratio
  • CI = Confidence Interval
