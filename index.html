Anti-diebetic Medication for Lung Disease

This dashboard provides insights into the relationship between anti-diabetic medications (GLP-1 Receptor Agonists, metformin, SGLT-2 inhibitors, DPP-4 inhibitors, and sulphonylureas) and lung disease. The data are based on a systematic review of the literature, and diabetes medications and lung disease. Use the filters to explore the data and visualise trends.

Data are based on the literature published up to July 2026. Search strategies are available elsewhere. The data will be regularly updated every 6 months.
